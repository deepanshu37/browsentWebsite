English Phrases

An intelligent language learning platform that helps non-native speakers master English phrases, idioms, and expressions used in real-world conversations. English Phrases uses spaced repetition and contextual learning to accelerate language acquisition.

Whether for business communication or everyday conversation, English Phrases makes learning natural, engaging, and effective.

  • Contextual phrase learning with real-world examples
  • Spaced repetition for optimal retention
  • Pronunciation guide with audio examples
  • Progress tracking and personalized learning paths
  • Business English and conversational modules
